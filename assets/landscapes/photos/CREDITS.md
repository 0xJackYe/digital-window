Viewpoint notes:

- Manhattan: photographed through a real window on the 21st floor of 32 Avenue of the Americas; this is an office building view, not a documented residential apartment.
- Prague: panorama of the real Old Town Square.
- Kyoto: real Yasaka-dori street and Yasaka Pagoda in the early morning; source lamps and shadows remain part of the photograph.
- Fuji: photographed from Hirano on Lake Yamanaka's eastern side. Its 6660 × 2055 panorama has fewer than 2160 vertical pixels and must not be advertised as native UHD 16:9.
- Lake: Moraine Lake, Banff National Park.
- Coast: Eriyadu in North Malé Atoll, Maldives; this is not Laamu Atoll.
- Forest: source photographer does not document a sufficiently precise shooting location; do not claim it was photographed in Beijing or another specific city.
